                                                Tenants
                                            

                                            
 The financial institutions that send us event data are our "customers".
                                                However, each customer can have different offerings, like wire, online
                                                banking, ach etc. In our system, we call these "tenants".
                                                


                                                For example, StarOne Credit Union is a customer and its different
                                                offerings viz. retail, wire, achrdfi etc are the different tenants. Each
                                                such tenant is allocated an id by us which is called a service id. A
                                                service id is unique inside a pod.
                                                In our system, a sample tenant name looks like this:
                                                


                                                StarOneCUCA-Retail_1777
                                                


                                                The part in red is called the host_name and the one in blue is called
                                                the host_service. The green is the service_id at the end.
                                                


                                                Each tenant has a tenant definition, which constitutes of the tenant
                                                model and the tenant configuration.
                                            

                                            
                                                                
                                        

                                        

                                            

                                                Configuration
                                            

                                            

                                                The tenants.pp files are how tenants show up here. The CommanderData
                                                program needs to be configured to watch the desired pod. Any
                                                tenants that are enabled will show up here and depending on the
                                                components enabled for them, can be managed through this tool.
                                            

                                            

                                                Enabled tenant pp example:
                                                

                                                fraudmap4::pod::tenant { "$pod_name:HostName-HostService_ServiceID":
                                                


                                                The CommanderData program is extremely picky about this line. If you
                                                mess it up, your tenant could not show up.
